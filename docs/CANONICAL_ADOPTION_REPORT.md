## 4. Owner-settlement result

ACCOUNTING26 and DATABASE7.6 select business-only owner custody, one pinned1070-family control per mixed channel. Crest's recorded net receipts remain an economic business asset held by the owner, separate from personal wealth. Customer settlement still credits AR, tips or customer funds; it is not owner contribution revenue. Funding-root lineage and physical custody lineage remain distinct.

The contract specifies receipts/embedded fees, expenses/AP dates, transfers/transit, personal appropriation/distributions, explicit contributions, personally funded costs/refunds/failures, genuine due-owner obligations, supplier refunds and exact reconciliation. No automatic equity-versus-liability choice: each personal funding event explicitly identifies contribution or a documented reimbursement obligation. No general due-from-owner loan is invented. Custody cannot be negative; a documented funding shortfall needs explicit financing intent, not a guessed debit/credit. Synthetic fixtures cover both allowed choices.

Custody reconciliation matches only business movements and explicit ownership designations, with exact opening/timing bridge and coverage attestation. It never reconciles against the owner's entire personal wallet/bank balance. Unknown coverage blocks clean reconciliation. This is a technical bookkeeping contract, not legal segregation, asset-protection advice or a tax classification.

## 7. Codex is forbidden to invent

- Taxability, registration, exemptions, rates, tax method/election, depreciation/capitalization policy or asset basis.
- Opening balances, verified zeros, historical sales/cash, cutover facts, personal account ownership, whole personal balances or fake bank movements.
- Automatic owner contributions, draws, loans, reimbursements or netting; per-event funding intent and real obligations require evidence.
- Refund/failure capacity from only initial receipt splits; new rounded components on reversal; automatic restoration of revenue from returned refunds.
- Used-control remapping, generic control journals, reconciliation plugs, fake timing items, cash to hide errors or silent prior-period restatement.
- New foundational posting templates for unsupported events; capture evidence and require a documented amendment.
- New scope from credits, amended accepted history, duplicate invoice numbers or automatic stale-command replay after restore.
- Automatic customer identity merges, accepted prices, cancellation fees, travel formulas, reminder cadence, insurance/legal sufficiency or messaging delivery.
- Missing costs as zero, complete lifetime/tax financial statements from partial records, or software test/production certification from this specification.
- Weaker security/backups/retention/export guarantees to hit a cost target, or speculative V1 features excluded by PRODUCT.
