# Payment correction review

November18 follow-through for the payment-correction finding in the November17 end-to-end design audit.

An authorized correction now shows a compact comparison before the existing explicit save: the selected invoice and payment date, correction date, amount still applied before/after, amount proposed for customer funds and the equal increase in the invoice balance. Exact payment/application references remain available in a keyboard-accessible disclosure. Human-readable civil dates retain their exact `time` datetime attributes; dates use the existing UTC civil-date display convention rather than conversion through the device zone.

The comparison is explicitly proposed until saved. It does not claim that cash moves, invent a total customer-funds balance or manufacture a live invoice balance. The original required reason, account-mapping/period/write-state gates, posting request, server validation, original reference, refusal, uncertainty, retirement and recovery behavior remain unchanged. Private review data still clears on suspension through the existing controller.

The display-only projection uses exact bigint cents and the server-reviewed remaining applied ranges. It sums the remaining portions for the before/after application comparison, but a proposed correction must fit the selected contiguous available portion. It cannot combine disconnected portions to display an unavailable correction as valid. Invalid, zero, negative, excessive and malformed amounts withhold the preview; the original submit validation remains authoritative.
