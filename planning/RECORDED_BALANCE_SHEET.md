# Recorded balance sheet

In the fictional workspace, open **Accounting → Reports → Fictional trial balance**, choose the reporting date, and load the report. Expand **Balance sheet** to see the recorded assets, liabilities, posted equity, and accumulated recorded-book earnings. An account opens the existing journal selector for that account and date. Journal pages reflect a fresh read.

The on-screen statement and CSV use the same authorized trial-balance snapshot. Assets retain debit-minus-credit balances, including negative wallets and accumulated depreciation. Liabilities and posted equity use credit-minus-debit balances. Accumulated earnings include the signed revenue, contra-revenue and expense balances once. An out-of-balance result is refused. The report adds no account, adjustment, or ledger write.

This implements the recorded as-of statement in ACCOUNTING §13. A dedicated period income statement and the split of accumulated earnings into prior and current book years remain open. The scope is fictional posted records with incomplete opening and reconciliation coverage. The snapshot metadata and exact signed cents are present in the CSV.
