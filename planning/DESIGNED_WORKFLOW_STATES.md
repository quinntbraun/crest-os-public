It does not certify an exhaustive fresh visual matrix: source inspection, registered tests, retained exact-source runs, current release checks and physical-device acceptance remain separate evidence. A failed or unexecuted branch stays unverified. Do not infer financial success from a loaded screen, a download request, or an absent original lookup.
